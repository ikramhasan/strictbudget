## Seed topics to research

Starting points from the product's edge. None of these is researched yet; they're places to look,
not targets.

- Zero based budgeting: how-tos, templates, examples, spreadsheets.
- Envelope budgeting: apps, methods, digital envelopes.
- Multi-currency budgeting: expats, budgeting in two currencies, foreign currency accounts.
- Budgeting as a couple or household: shared budgets, splitting bills.
- Self-hosted and open-source budgeting apps.
- Moving off YNAB: alternatives, YNAB vs …, importing a YNAB budget.
- Credit cards in a budget, sinking funds, irregular income.
