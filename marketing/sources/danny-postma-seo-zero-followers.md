# How to Build a Successful Product with 0 Followers

**Author:** Danny Postma ([@dannypostma](https://x.com/dannypostma))
**Posted:** April 13, 2023
**Source:** https://x.com/dannypostma/status/1646368426246680579

> "You're only successful because you have a large following."
> BS. Here's how you can do the same with 0 followers.

**Case study:** [figcomponents.com](http://figcomponents.com), a product never mentioned on Twitter that makes about **$1,200/month**.

---

## Step 1: Find Your Main Keyword 🔍

Use the **Ahrefs Free Keyword Generator**. Keep searching until you find a keyword with:

- **Keyword Difficulty (KD) under 20**
- **Search volume over 500**
- **Many child keywords**

### Why these criteria

- **Keyword Difficulty:** KD < 20 needs fewer than 10 backlinks to rank on page one of Google.
- **Volume:** The figure is for one country, so triple it for worldwide. 500 × 3 = 1,500 searches; at a 2% conversion rate that's ~30 sales/month.
- **Child keywords:** Ranking for the main keyword means you also rank for its long-tail keywords, and that's where the money is. Figcomponents ranks for 1,000+ keywords, including "figma components," which brings in 95% of its traffic.

> ⚠️ **This is the most important step.** You'll spend months on this keyword, so take a day or two to find the best one.

---

## Step 2: Build Your Product Around the Keyword

1. **Give users what they want.** Deliver it immediately so visitors stay longer, which Google likes. (Figcomponents curated a free Figma Community library into an easy-to-copy component gallery, with zero investment.)
2. **Optimize for your main keyword.** Use it in your domain, title and alt text, but don't overstuff it, since Google penalizes that.
3. **Build as many subpages as possible.** Create pages targeting each long-tail keyword. Link the most important ones from the home page and all of them from a category page so Google can find them.
4. **Offer both free and paid options.** Free tools keep users on the site (boosting rankings) and build trust. Gate some tools behind a **one-time payment**, which converts better.

---

## Step 3: Get Backlinks 🔗

Backlinks are Google's #1 ranking factor: more links signal a better product.

- **Launch on Product Hunt.** Your ranking there doesn't matter; even last place wins, because you get a link from a domain with DR 90/100.
- **Syndication bonus.** Many sites repost Product Hunt launches, so you typically get **10–50 backlinks**, not one. That's enough to reach page one for a KD < 10 keyword.

---

## Step 4: Wait ⏳

- Google takes about **3 months** to start sending meaningful traffic.
- Expect a larger boost after roughly **another 6 months**.
- Have all your pages ready up front so you don't waste that time.

---

## Takeaways

- This builds a product with **0 followers and a $0 marketing budget**.
- It's largely **set-and-forget**: traffic keeps coming once you rank.
- An audience only speeds up link building, giving you a head start of a few months over competitors. That's it.
